You can access a wide range of English texts for all levels, from beginner to advanced, including short stories, classic literature, and practical everyday materials, often with comprehension exercises. Beginner and Elementary Texts For learners starting out, websites like Lingua offer short, simple texts designed to improve vocabulary and grammar comprehension. These include everyday topics such as school, hobbies, holidays, and family life, often accompanied by multiple-choice quizzes to test understanding. Texts can be downloaded as PDFs for offline practice. The British Council also provides A1-level texts like posters, messages, forms, and timetables to help learners understand simple information.
